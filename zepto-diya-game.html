Zepto hands you a diya to light

A diya-lighting game with rewards sits beside curated festive sections for gifts, lights and food.

D

Design Duniya
9 Nov 2026  ·  1 min read

Quick-commerce apps have a problem that festivals make worse: people only open them when they need something. Zepto’s answer this Diwali was to hand shoppers a diya and ask them to light it.

Social Samosa reports that the app launched a diya-lighting game that pays out rewards, alongside curated festive sections for gifts, diyas and lights, and food. The publication reads games like this one as a way to turn ordering into play and to give people a reason to come back.

The choice of ritual is the smart part. Lighting a diya is the smallest, most familiar act of the festival, something every user has done with their own hands. A game built on it needs no tutorial.

The usual caution applies. A reward mechanic can tip from charming to nagging quickly, and the report does not describe how often the game appears or what the rewards are. Alongside it, Zepto ran a simpler offer: a free box of Haldiram’s mithai with orders placed from 6 to 8 November.

What changed

A diya-lighting game with rewards sits beside curated festive sections for gifts, lights and food.

Why it works

  • Lighting a diya needs no tutorial.
  • A game gives a reason to open the app with nothing to buy.
  • Rewards tie the play back to an order.
